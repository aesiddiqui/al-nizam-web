al-Nizam — Noon (ن) logo concepts

Draft exploration · 2026-06-28 · in-house (Claude design) · DRAFT — civilizational grounding pending al-'Alim consult before public use

The Arabic letter Noon (ن) is a deep bowl with a dot above — and that shape is the thesis: the bowl is the structure that holds (continuity, governance, accountability); the dot is the operator, held above it. Every concept below is that idea, in a different voice. All are monochrome by design (a mark must work in one color first); accent options shown at the bottom.

1 · Vessel — monoline, geometric minimal

The purest reading: a clean single-weight bowl + dot. Calm, modern, infinitely scalable, reads instantly as Noon. The "default" — confident and unfussy. Best favicon behaviour.
