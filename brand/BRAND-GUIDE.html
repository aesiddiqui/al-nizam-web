01 — The Mark

A planet on an orbit

The mark is the Arabic letter Noon (ن) read as celestial mechanics: a tilted orbital band with a single body resting on it.

It carries the meaning of the framework in one silhouette. The orbit is al-Nizam — the order that holds everything in relation. The dot is the Steward — you — held on that orbit, free to move because the structure is doing the holding. Nizam means order, system, arrangement; the mark says it without a word of explanation.

The dot is a plain dot — deliberately. The Confidant is present as the structure that holds the dot on its orbit, not drawn as a second element. A Saturn-ring variant was explored and set aside to keep the mark singular and reducible. (See logo-saturn-v2.html for that exploration.)

Clear space & construction

The mark is drawn on a centered 90×90 viewBox (viewBox="56.5 66.7 90 90") with the geometry already balanced inside it. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the dot on all sides. Never place type or graphics inside that margin. Minimum size: 16px (the plain dot holds down to favicon scale; the wordmark drops below ~24px — use the mark alone).

02 — Logo Variants

One mark, every surface

Pick the variant by background, never by preference. The mark is monochrome and themeable (fill="currentColor") — it inherits the surface's text color unless a brand color is explicitly set.

Horizontal lockup

Mark + wordmark, optionally with the tight tagline. The wordmark is set in a serif (Georgia shown as placeholder — see §4). The mark sits at cap-height of the wordmark with one mark-width of space between them.

Wordmark casing: always al-Nizam — lowercase al-, capital N, hyphenated, one word after. Never "Al Nizam", "AlNizam", or "al nizam".

04 — Typography

Serif for the name, sans for the work

A two-voice system: a serif carries the wordmark and headings — it gives al-Nizam the settled, authored, institutional weight the brand claims. A clean sans-serif carries everything functional: body copy, UI, labels.

RoleTypefaceNotes
Wordmark & headingsSerif — Georgia (placeholder)Final display serif is the design-system step pending
Body & UISans-serif — system / Inter-classRegular for copy, Medium for subheads, Semibold for buttons
Code / tokensMonospaceHex values, file paths, technical references

Georgia is a placeholder so the system is legible today without a web-font dependency. Selecting the final display serif (and confirming the body sans) is a deliberate type step before public ship — candidates should be evaluated against the wordmark at small and large sizes, light and dark.

06 — Do & Don't

Protecting the mark

✓ Do

  • Use at 100% opacity, full strength
  • Keep clear space (= dot height) on all sides
  • Pick the variant by background contrast
  • Keep the wordmark as al-Nizam — exact casing & hyphen
  • Let the mark inherit color via currentColor where possible

✕ Don't

  • Stretch, compress, rotate, or skew the geometry
  • Recolor outside the palette, or add gradients/shadows/outlines
  • Place the full mark on a busy or low-contrast background
  • Reduce opacity or use the mark as a faint watermark behind text
  • Re-letter, re-space, or set the wordmark in a non-brand face for logo use
  • Crop the orbit or detach the dot from the band
