01

Brand foundation

al‑Nizam is the layer between an operator and their AI tools — the place where continuity, governance, and accountability live structurally, in files the operator controls.

The name

Arabic for the order, the system, the discipline. Styled with a capital N — al‑Nizam. The name is always set in Spectral, never abbreviated, and never written “Al Nizam” or “AlNizam.”

The audience

AI‑forward operators, engineering and IT leaders, and founders — people who need their systems to hold their thinking so they can do more of it.

Principles

Ordered

Structure before flourish. Every element sits on a grid, in a system, for a reason.

Enduring

Classical, not trendy. Built to read the same in a decade as it does today.

Accountable

Confident and plain‑spoken. We show the mechanism; we don't hide behind it.

02

The mark

A tapered crescent bowl carrying its dot — the Arabic letter Noon (ن). One form, read three ways at once.

ن

As a letter

The crescent is the bowl of the Noon; the orb is its dot, held in the classic position above the curve.

As meaning

The orb is the Steward; the curve that cradles it, the framework and families that hold continuity in orbit.

As heritage

A crescent and orb — golden‑age astronomy — echoing the framework's own al‑Mushahada, observation.

03

Construction & clear space

The clear space around the mark equals the diameter of the dot — one X. Keep this margin free of type, imagery, and other marks.

X X X X

Minimum clear space = 1X on every side, where X is the dot diameter.

Proportion rules

—

The dot sits above the crescent's inner peak and never touches it.

—

The mark is drawn from a fixed vector. Never redraw, re‑space, or re‑weight it.

—

Only two colors ever appear in the mark: one for the crescent, one for the dot.

—

In one‑color contexts, the dot takes the same color as the crescent.

04

Minimum size & misuse

The mark holds down to 16 px on screen and 6 mm in print. Below that, legibility of the dot fails — use the solid favicon build instead.

09

Voice & tone

Confident, plain‑spoken, and structural. We explain the mechanism and let it earn trust — no hype, no mysticism.

We say

“Continuity you own, in files you control.”

“Built so you can think.”

“The system holds the state, so you don't have to.”

We avoid

“Revolutionary AI magic.”

“Supercharge your workflow!”

“The future is here.”

Ordered

Lead with the structure. Name the parts.

Precise

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No filler.

Assured

Calm authority. We don't oversell.
