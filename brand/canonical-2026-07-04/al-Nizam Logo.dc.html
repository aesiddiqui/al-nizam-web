The mark

A single Noon, read three ways.

A tapered crescent bowl carrying its dot — the letter Noon (ن). The same form reads as a letter, as meaning, and as heritage.

ن

As a letter

The crescent is the bowl of the Noon; the orb is its dot, holding the classic position above the curve.

As meaning

The orb is the Steward; the curve that cradles it, the framework and families that hold continuity in orbit.

As heritage

A crescent and orb — golden‑age astronomy — echoing the framework's own al‑Mushahada, observation.
